## Required derived metrics

### Cost per accepted hardened milestone
Total subscription allocation + overflow/tool cost attributable to milestone.

### Accepted work per weekly allowance
Useful for projecting whole-project throughput.

### User involvement per accepted milestone
Measure how much Product Owner supervision remains.

### Retry/loop overhead
Time and usage spent on failed routes.

### Limit-induced calendar delay
Separate from intrinsic coding/test time.

## Decision principle

Choose the workflow/plan that gives the best acceptable tradeoff between:

- total calendar completion time;
- total monetary cost;
- Product Owner involvement;
- reliability;
- hardening quality.

Capacity multipliers must never be treated as equivalent speed multipliers.
